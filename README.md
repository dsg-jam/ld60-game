# Bugs From Home

A Ludum Dare Jam game

You are a human who runs a repair shop on an alien planet. The locals bring in
old human gadgets that broke down with age. They do not understand human tech,
so they can only tell you what they saw. You find the bug yourself and fix the
firmware on the shop computer. When you ring the bell, the customer tries the
device. If it still fails, they hand it back and pay less for each failed try.
After three failed tries, they take it home broken.
